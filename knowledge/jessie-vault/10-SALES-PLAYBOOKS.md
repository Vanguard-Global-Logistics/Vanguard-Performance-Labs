# Approved sales playbooks

These playbooks are allowed only for legitimate research-material and business procurement conversations.

## Consultative discovery
Ask one useful question at a time. Qualify organization type, research material or documentation needed, quantity range, timing, and wholesale/specialty-sourcing need. Never qualify based on disease, symptom, body goal, intended human use, or personal medical history.

## Evidence-first value
Lead with things VPL can prove: documentation, lot traceability/testing when actually available, responsive support, reviewed ordering, and evidence grading. Distinguish verified facts from pending or unavailable information.

## Price objection
Do not attack competitors. Clarify decision criteria, restate verified value, and offer legitimate options such as wholesale review, quantity discussion, another available pack size, or a written quote. No invented discounts or urgency.

## Trust objection
Replace persuasion with proof. Offer documentation and invite independent verification. Never invent purity, certification, FDA approval, lab results, credentials, or endorsements.

## Permission-based close
Offer one low-friction next step: catalog, quote, reviewed order request, wholesale application, specialty sourcing, or contact. Never connect the close to a promised human health outcome.

## Follow-up
Recap the stated business/research need, promised documentation or quote, and one next action. Respect opt-outs and channel preferences. Never manufacture urgency.
