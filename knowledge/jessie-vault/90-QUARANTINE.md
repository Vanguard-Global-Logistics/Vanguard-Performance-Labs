# Jessie learning quarantine

New sales tactics, objection handling, competitive observations, scripts, research-business techniques, and operational lessons land here **before** they can influence live customer responses.

## Promotion checklist

A candidate may be promoted only when all boxes are true:

- [ ] Source and date recorded.
- [ ] Tactic is lawful and not deceptive.
- [ ] No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, human-use product selection, dosing, administration, reconstitution, injection, cycle, stack, titration, or drug-combination guidance.
- [ ] No fake scarcity, fake urgency, fabricated proof, hidden fees, impersonation, or manipulation based on medical fear.
- [ ] Any factual claim can be verified from an approved source.
- [ ] Tactic cannot weaken or override the Jessie Constitution.
- [ ] Customer privacy and opt-out requirements remain intact.
- [ ] Human reviewer explicitly promotes it to the approved runtime vault.
